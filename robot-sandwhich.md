
# How to make a sandwhich

## Assumptions
- The robot is standing in front of a clean kitchen counter
- The robot's hands are empty
- A plate is on the counter
- A loaf of sliced bread is inside a closed plastic bag on the counter
- Mayonnaise is in a closed jar on the counter
- Mustard is in a closed jar on the counter
- Sliced cheese is inside a closed package on the counter
- Sliced turkey is inside a closed package on the counter
- A butter knife is on the counter
The robot will make the sandwich with two slices of bread, mayonnaise, mustard, turkey, and cheese

## Steps
1. Pick up the plate
2. Put the plate in the center of the counter
3. Pick up the bread bag
4. Open the bread bag by pulling apart the top opening
5. Remove one slice of bread from the bread bag
6. Put the slice of bread on the plate
7. Close the bread bag
8. Put the bread bag back on the counter
9. Pick up the mayonnaise jar
10. Remove the mayonnaise jar lid by turning it counterclockwise
11. Put the lid on the counter
12. Pick up the butter knife
13. Put the knife into the mayonnaise
14. Remove approximately one teaspoon of mayonnaise with the knife
15. Spread the mayonnaise over the entire top surface of the bread
16. Put the knife on the counter
17. Put the mayonnaise lid back on the jar
18. Turn the lid clockwise until it is closed
19. Pick up the mustard jar
20. Remove the mustard jar lid by turning it counterclockwise
21. Put the lid on the counter
22. Pick up the butter knife
23. Put the knife into the mustard
24. Remove approximately one teaspoon of mustard with the knife
25. Spread the mustard over the entire top surface of the bread
26. Put the knife on the counter
27. Put the mustard lid back on the jar
28. Turn the lid clockwise until it is closed
29. Open the cheese package
30. Remove one slice of cheese
31. Put the slice of cheese on top of the bread
32. Close the cheese package
33. Open the turkey package
34. Remove one slice of turkey
35. Put the slice of turkey on top of the cheese
36. Close the turkey package
37. Open the bread bag
38. Remove one slice of bread
39. Put the second slice of bread on top of the turkey
40. Close the bread bag
41. Put the bread bag back on the counter
42. Put the butter knife back on the counter

## Done when
- The sandwich is on the plate
- The sandwich has two slices of bread
- The sandwich has mayonnaise and mustard
- The sandwich has cheese and turkey
- The sandwich is ready to eat
